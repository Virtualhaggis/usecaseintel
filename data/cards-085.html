User received an external email containing a URL, clicked it, and within 60 seconds the recipient's endpoint spawned a scripting interpreter or network downloader (PowerShell / cmd / mshta / curl / certutil / bitsadmin). This is the high-fidelity shape — a raw "user clicked an emailed URL" query is too noisy to alert on because every Microsoft 365 tenant generates thousands of legitimate clicks per day.
