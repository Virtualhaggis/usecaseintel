[LLM] BoltDB Go backdoor C2 callback to 49.12.198.231:20022 Command & Control High
Hunts the hardcoded reverse-shell C2 used by the malicious github.com/boltdb-go/bolt v1.3.1 Go module. The backdoor's ApiInit() in db.go reconstructs the C2 address from obfuscated constants in cursor.go and connects to 49.12.198.231 TCP/20022 (Hetzner AS24940). Any outbound connection to this IP/port pair is a high-fidelity indicator a host has executed a Go binary built against the backdoored module. Rationale: The C2 IP 49.12.198.231:20022 is a hardcoded indicator extracted from cursor.go's obfuscated MaxMemSize/MaxIndex/MaxPort constants and confirmed by Socket.dev's primary analysis. IP+port together are zero-FP for any organization that isn't running their own service on that pair. Cross-checked against: • https://socket.dev/blog/malicious-package-exploits-go-module-proxy-caching-for-persistence • https://pkg.go.dev/vuln/GO-2025-3451 • https://thehackernews.com/2025/02/malicious-go-package-exploits-module.html
