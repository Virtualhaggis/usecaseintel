Phishing-link click correlated to endpoint execution Delivery High
User received an external email containing a URL, clicked it, and within 60 seconds the recipient's endpoint spawned a scripting interpreter or network downloader (PowerShell / cmd / mshta / curl / certutil / bitsadmin).
