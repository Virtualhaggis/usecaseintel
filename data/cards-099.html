SourMint/Mintegral SDK covert click-data exfiltration to n.systemlog.me AIActions on Objectives High
Hunts network egress to the SourMint exfiltration endpoint n.systemlog.me/log, where the malicious Mintegral iOS SDK POSTs custom-encoded payloads (IDFA/IDFV, intercepted openURL clicks, headers).
