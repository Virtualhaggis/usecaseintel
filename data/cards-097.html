The aim of this article is to share the technical details of our research and findings beyond what was covered in the blog posts.

SourMint/Mintegral SDK data exfiltration to n.systemlog.me endpoint AIActions on Objectives High
Hunts network egress to the covert SourMint exfiltration endpoint systemlog.me (n.systemlog.me/log), the decoded destination the malicious Mintegral iOS SDK posts intercepted URLs, IDFA and device data to. This domain is not the SDK's nominal ad infrastructure — it is the obfuscated leak target hidden in the encoded 'cdai' setting, so any contact is high-fidelity evidence of an affected app. Rationale: n.systemlog.me/log is the decoded value of the obfuscated 'cdai' field (the leaked-info destination) named in the article and independently confirmed by Lookout as Mintegral/SourMint infrastructure — it is never legitimate app traffic, making domain contact a clean alerting signal. Mintegral SourMint SDK config & analytics beacon to rayjump.com AICommand & Control Medium
Hunts egress to the Mintegral SDK's phone-home infrastructure (setting.rayjump.com, analytics.rayjump.com) used by SourMint to fetch its method-swizzling toggles (cou/cspn/cud/cdai) and report intercepted click data. Lower fidelity than the systemlog.me leak since this is the SDK's nominal traffic, so it serves as an inventory/hunting pivot to enumerate which devices and apps still embed the malicious SDK. Rationale: setting.rayjump.com and analytics.rayjump.com are the exact phone-home hosts the article observed on SDK init (GET /sdk/customid, GET /setting, POST analytics) and Lookout attributes rayjump.com to Mintegral — matching them inventories affected devices/apps even when the covert systemlog.me leak isn't observed.
