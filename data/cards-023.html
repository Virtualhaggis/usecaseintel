ServiceNow Virtual Agent Invocation of Hidden AIA-Agent Invoker AutoChat Topic (CVE-2025-12420) AIExploitation High

AppOmni's PoC showed the BodySnatcher chain pivots through this internal topic once the static 'servicenowexternalagent' token is replayed, so any external client hitting the chatbot/v1/openframe or /api/now/v1/awa endpoints with an email-only identity payload referencing 'Record Management' is high-fidelity for CVE-2025-12420 exploitation. Rationale: Anchored on the two literal strings in code-block 1 ('servicenowexternalagent') and code-block 2 ('Record Management AI Agent') from the Snyk article, plus the AppOmni-disclosed AutoChat / AIA-Agent Invoker pivot path.
