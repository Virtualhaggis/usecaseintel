Hunts the canonical Log4Shell exploit primitive — a `${jndi:ldap://}` (or obfuscated `${lower:}`/`%24%7b` variant) JNDI lookup string reflected into a logged HTTP field such as the URI query, User-Agent, or other header that a vulnerable Apache Log4j 2.x instance would log and resolve.
